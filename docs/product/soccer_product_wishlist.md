# SoccerTrainingApp — Product Wishlist

Wishlist items are not release commitments and are not permission to build or spend. Promote an item only after observed demand, scoped design, security/rights review where relevant, cost/capacity review and explicit acceptance criteria.

## W01 — Automated technique scoring

Potential value: faster self-feedback and measurable improvement.

Risks: false confidence, age bias, device/camera variability, ball occlusion, unsafe coaching claims, privacy and model-support cost.

Promotion trigger: reliable benchmark on representative devices and ages, qualified coaching validation, error-rate thresholds and a safe fallback that never blocks normal training.

## W02 — Live rotatable 3D demonstrations

Potential value: improved spatial understanding.

Risks: mobile performance, production complexity, larger assets and limited evidence that it improves outcomes over high-quality pre-rendered views.

Promotion trigger: user research shows a meaningful comprehension benefit over alternate-angle pre-rendered clips.

## W03 — Public/community social features

Potential value: motivation and community.

Risks: child safety, moderation, privacy, harassment, unhealthy comparison and operating cost.

Promotion trigger: strong demand plus a dedicated safeguarding/moderation model. Public child profiles and unrestricted messaging remain out of scope by default.

## W04 — Leaderboards and competitive gamification

Potential value: repeat use.

Risks: pressure, unhealthy minutes targets, gaming the metric and discouraging younger/beginner players.

Promotion trigger: evidence that private/healthy goals are insufficient and a reviewed design can avoid harmful incentives.

## W05 — Talent identification / scouting

Potential value: aspirational differentiation.

Risks: validity, fairness, child-development harm, legal/reputational exposure and misleading career claims.

Promotion trigger: independent validated methodology and explicit specialist/legal review. Do not infer talent from generic engagement metrics.

## W06 — Coach marketplace / paid mentoring

Potential value: monetization and higher-touch feedback.

Risks: safeguarding, vetting, tax/payment/refund complexity, workload guarantees and adult–child communication boundaries.

Promotion trigger: validated demand, coach supply model, vetting process, safeguarding design and unit economics.

## W07 — Advanced club integrations

Examples: roster imports, calendar integrations, SSO, team-management platform connectors.

Promotion trigger: pilot clubs repeatedly identify manual setup as a material blocker after core roster/invitation flows are stable.

## W08 — Smart-ball / wearable integrations

Potential value: richer objective data.

Risks: hardware dependency, fragmented vendors, support burden and cost.

Promotion trigger: a target segment already owns compatible hardware and the integration materially improves the training proposition.

## W09 — AI-generated coaching plans

Potential value: personalization and content scale.

Risks: unsafe or age-inappropriate plans, hallucinated drill advice, rights/content governance and unclear coaching accountability.

Promotion trigger: use constrained retrieval over coach-approved curriculum, explicit guardrails and qualified review rather than open-ended generation.

## W10 — Independent Coach Pro product

Potential value: paid tools for coaches outside club subscriptions.

Promotion trigger: coach workflow pilot demonstrates independent demand and support economics without weakening family/club permission boundaries.

## W11 — Extra cloud storage add-ons

Potential value: monetization and retention for video-heavy households/clubs.

Promotion trigger: observed quota pressure after compression/retention choices are validated and users understand local-vs-cloud state clearly.

## W12 — Unattended multi-agent dispatcher

Potential value: founder leverage and routine maintenance automation.

Risks: scope drift, duplicated actions, spend, secret exposure and self-approval.

Promotion trigger: bounded single-agent workflows are mature, evidence contracts are reliable, CI/review protections are proven and stop/spend rules are enforceable.
