# ACT-SP-155-01 — Optional goals and private milestones

| Field | Recommended pilot value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 recommended for owner acceptance |
| Default | No weekly goal; no reminder |
| Optional goal choices | 1, 2 or 3 completed practice sessions per local week, within an approved pathway |
| Visibility | Exact player and authorized guardian/adult only; coach sees only when separately granted |

An authorized adult selects or disables the weekly goal. It never changes the
coach-approved session content, duration, work/rest, ability or next-session
decision. A player can practise with no goal and receives the same content and
core access.

`completed` means the app safely finalized a started practice record; it is not
a technique, effort or performance judgment. Stopping early, recording nothing,
resting or selecting an easier approved alternative never produces punishment,
lost access, a negative score or pressure to compensate later.

Milestones are private statements of completed practice count such as `First
practice saved` or `Three practices completed`. They are not public badges,
talent levels, competitive points, rankings or efficacy claims. Earned
milestones survive breaks, opt-out, missed weeks and goal changes. Deletion of
the underlying history follows SP-061 and may remove the derived milestone.

Supportive copy:

- `Nice to see you. Choose a practice that fits today.`
- `Stopping was the right choice if something didn't feel safe.`
- `Rest is part of training. Your progress is still here.`
- `No goal selected. You can practise whenever it suits you.`
- `This week's goal wasn't completed. Nothing has been lost.`

No streak countdown, shame, public comparison, points economy, competitive
minutes target, recording requirement or automatic difficulty increase exists.
