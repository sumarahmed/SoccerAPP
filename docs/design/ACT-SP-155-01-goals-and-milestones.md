# ACT-SP-155-01 — Optional goals and private milestones

| Field | Accepted pilot value |
|---|---|
| Version | `sp155-v1` |
| Status | Accepted product/coaching design baseline |
| Accountable owner | Syed Ahmed, founder/product owner |
| Coaching approver | Aaron M, A Diploma |
| Approval date | 16 September 2026 |
| Default | No weekly goal and no reminder |
| Goal choices | 1, 2 or 3 recorded practice sessions per local week; maximum three |
| Authority | Authorized guardian or adult player; coach may suggest but cannot impose/edit |
| Visibility | Exact player and authorized adult only; coach only under a separate explicit grant |

Goal choice never changes an approved session's content, duration, work/rest,
ability, pathway progression or SP-152 recommendation. A player with no goal
has identical training access. No goal requires recording or upload.

## Session counting

A session contributes once when the player enters active practice, the session
is safely finalized/stored and its stable session ID has not already counted.
Opening a detail/countdown screen or cancelling before active practice does not
count. A safe early stop after active practice began counts as participation so
the goal never encourages continuing through discomfort or unsafe conditions.

Participation is separate from assignment completion, attendance, technique and
assessed ability. Retried writes, offline synchronization, device reconnection
and duplicate events cannot count one session twice.

## Goal changes and missed weeks

- Disabling a goal cancels future goal reminders without changing training access.
- Changing between 1, 2 and 3 preserves participation already recorded that week.
- Increasing a goal sends no additional pressure message or catch-up workload.
- Lowering a goal may satisfy it but creates no retroactive reward.
- A missed week creates no debt, negative score, make-up work or content change.
- Rest, no recording and choosing an exact easier approved alternative never cause punishment.

## Private milestones

Milestones are factual private statements such as `First practice recorded`,
`Three practices recorded` and `Ten practices recorded`. They are generated
idempotently and survive breaks, missed weeks, opt-out and goal changes. Lawful
deletion of underlying history recomputes affected derived milestones; an old
offline event cannot restore deleted history or a removed milestone.

Milestones never unlock access, change difficulty or claim technique or health
improvement. There are no public badges, leaderboards, percentile comparisons,
streak-loss deadlines, points, monetary rewards or professional-potential claims.

## Supportive copy

- `A Soccolo practice is available when it suits your family.`
- `Rest is part of training. Your progress is still here.`
- `Stopping was the right choice if something didn't feel safe.`
- `No goal selected. You can practise whenever it suits you.`
- `This week has ended. Nothing has been lost.`
