## Searches performed

Public image-result searches were run for `double C logo dot symbol app brand`, `two lowercase c logo sports training brand`, `CC monogram logo same direction`, `inward facing double C dot logo`, `mirrored C dot C app logo` and `two C shapes facing each other centre dot logo`. The result set showed a crowded field of stock and bespoke double-C monograms, especially mirrored and interlocking constructions. It also surfaced Cuepri's current mark, which uses two inward-facing green curved forms around a green centre dot. The full Soccolo wordmark remains materially different as a whole, but the standalone v0.2 device shares the same dominant arrangement. A public-web screen is incomplete and cannot establish clearance.

Official guidance consulted:

- [IP Australia — How to search existing trade marks](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/search-existing-trade-marks) confirms that Australian Trade Mark Search and TM Checker support image comparison, and that similarity and distinctiveness are examined.
- [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) supports image-similarity searches but advises checking national/regional registers as well.
- [WIPO availability guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/check-availability) advises expanding beyond exact matches to marks similar in appearance, sound or meaning and related goods/services.

## High-salience risks

| Reference category | Similar feature | Current differentiation | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Cuepri](https://cuepri.com/about-the-cuepri-team) behavioral-health software/platform mark | Two inward-facing green curved forms around a green centre dot | Soccolo uses fuller custom `c` shapes, mint-on-navy/deep-green colour roles and the custom `Soccolo` wordmark | **High-risk for standalone use:** the dominant symbol arrangement is close and both brands operate software/platform services, even though the fields and names differ. Do not approve the app/device symbol without professional review or redesign. |
| Creative Commons `CC` marks | Two `c` characters used together | Soccolo uses two inward-facing forms, a centre touch point, one accent colour and a custom full wordmark; it is not enclosed in the Creative Commons circle | Material concern for symbol-only use. Creative Commons' published policy identifies `CC` standing alone and its circle logo as marks. Avoid any circle enclosure and obtain professional assessment. |
| Interlocking luxury/fashion double-C marks | Repeated letter C | The candidate does not mirror or interlock the forms and does not use opposing hooks | Lower structural similarity, but keep the forms non-interlocking. |
| Stock CC monograms | Generic repeated C geometry | Candidate uses custom paths, asymmetric wordmark context, a touch point and project colours | Crowded-field risk remains; stock-search non-match is not clearance. |
| Collaboration/link/infinity app symbols | Paired circular forms | Candidate Cs face inward but remain separated by a solid touch point and do not interlock | The selected geometry increases this category's visual proximity compared with v0.1; official image-search evidence remains required. |

Creative Commons' [Trademark Policy](https://creativecommons.org/policies/) is especially relevant because it expressly treats `CC` standing alone and the CC-in-a-circle logo as Creative Commons marks. The Soccolo candidate must never be placed inside a circle or described simply as the “CC logo.”

## Required clearance work before launch approval

1. Upload cropped symbol-only and combined wordmark PNGs to IP Australia TM Checker/ATMS image comparison; inspect related classes and save result identifiers/screenshots.
2. Manually upload the same files to WIPO's Global Brand Database using concept, shape and composite strategies; WIPO prohibits automated bulk querying, so this must be an interactive recorded search.
3. Repeat against EUIPO/TMview and the national registers of each planned launch territory.
4. Search unregistered/common-law uses, app stores, domains and sports/education products using both `Soccolo` and visual variants.
5. Obtain professional trade-mark advice on the standalone symbol and combined name/device mark before filing or irreversible launch investment.
6. Retain the selected Concept E treatment inside the full wordmark for refinement, but replace or materially differentiate the app/device symbol unless professional review concludes the Cuepri and other cited risks are acceptable in the intended territories/classes.

The current evidence supports continued internal review of the complete `Soccolo` wordmark only. It does not support a “no conflicts found,” registrable, non-infringing or launch-ready conclusion, and it affirmatively identifies a close standalone-device candidate requiring resolution.
