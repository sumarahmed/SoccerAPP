## Construction record

The selected generated board and founder-supplied crop use mirrored `c` forms opening inward around a mint centre point. Version 0.1 incorrectly changed that defining relationship to two same-direction forms. Version 0.2 corrects the deviation: one custom `c` path is mirrored mathematically around the touch point, and the entire double-touch unit uses the accent colour. No pixel edge, contour or path was auto-traced. The remaining Soccolo wordmark is a purpose-built monoline geometric drawing; the theme is rendered with a small custom vector alphabet. Colour values come from existing project tokens.

The build and render scripts are retained beside the assets so each SVG can be reproduced and reviewed. The asset manifest records SHA-256 values for the exact outputs.

## Rights position

No third-party artwork or font file is present in the controlled candidate. That materially reduces component-licensing risk but does not prove copyright subsistence, uniqueness, trade-mark registrability or non-infringement.

Under the [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/), as between the user and OpenAI and to the extent permitted by law, the user owns Output and OpenAI assigns any rights it has; the terms also state that output may not be unique. Australia continues to examine legal certainty for AI-generated materials, and other jurisdictions may require sufficient human authorship for copyright protection. The final record should therefore preserve Syed Ahmed's selection, requested changes and exact approval, and should obtain a material human design refinement or specialist advice if stronger copyright exclusivity is commercially important.

Trade-mark risk is separate from copyright. `SIMILARITY-REVIEW.md` records a preliminary screen and a specific warning about the standalone inward-facing `C·C` reading. Restoring the selected geometry improves design fidelity but does not reduce legal risk. Do not publish or file the standalone device until the required official-register image searches and professional assessment are complete.
