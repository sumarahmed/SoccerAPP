## 2. Binding decisions

1. Safety/help is prominent across practice, feedback, media, account and error
   routes; immediate Stop never depends on reporting, connectivity or recording.
2. Reports use structured categories with optional short text and attachment;
   conflict-safe routing never automatically sends details to the subject.
3. A named human safeguarding owner and independent alternate are mandatory
   before real children participate. An AI/agent cannot fill those roles.
4. Critical acknowledgment within one hour applies only during declared staffed
   pilot windows. Soccolo does not promise continuous monitoring.
5. Protection is narrow and attributable and does not delete evidence, disable
   the whole account or block Stop/safe local practice by default.
6. Web targets WCAG 2.2 AA and native clients equivalent behavior, including
   screen reader, switch/keyboard, contrast, scaling, motion and target sizing.
7. Recording is optional for safe practice; declined camera/microphone access
   and missing variants have usable, honest alternatives.
8. Every released drill/asset needs exact version, rights, coach, safeguarding,
   accessibility, variant and device evidence. No silent substitution is allowed.
9. D01–D12 remain blocked; D12 additionally needs a goalkeeper reviewer.
   Heading, contact, collision/diving and overhead work remain excluded.
10. SP-005, SP-007 and SP-038 dependencies remain visible and prevent this
    family from being treated as pilot-readiness evidence.

## 3. Accepted risks and limits

- Human owners, jurisdictional duties and specialist review remain unfilled.
- No real assistive-technology, device, child-comprehension or operational test
  was performed.
- No content, screen or real child-data release is authorized by this decision.
- The evolving Australian Children's Online Privacy Code must be revisited.
- Owner acceptance is not legal, safeguarding, coaching or accessibility
  certification.
