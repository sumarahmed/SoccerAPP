## Accepted decisions

1. **First market and language:** The first market is Australia and the first supported language is English.
2. **Initial pilot age representation:** The pilot target is 20 participants across five design bands: four participants aged 5–7, four aged 8–10, four aged 11–13, four aged 14–17, and four aged 18. This is a representation and recruitment target, not evidence that participants have been recruited.
3. **Solo and assisted expectations:** Solo drills are the default. Players aged 5–7 use adult-nearby setup and support. Older players operate independently when the drill, their ability and the circumstances are suitable. Any drill requiring a partner or adult assistance must say so explicitly.
4. **Adult account treatment:** Accounts for players under 18 remain guardian-controlled. At age 18, the player becomes a self-managing adult and explicitly decides whether existing family access continues. Payment, club membership or a prior family relationship does not automatically preserve access.
5. **Recording modes:** The product supports both grouped exercise clips and full-session recording with chapter markers. The recording mode is selected before recording. Full-session recording includes programmed rests; the chapter model must not require duplicate source recordings.
