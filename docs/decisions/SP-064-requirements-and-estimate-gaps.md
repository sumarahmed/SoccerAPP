Syed Ahmed accepted the recommendation to reconcile current requirements,
content quantities and estimate gaps without contacting suppliers, changing
the workbook, funding a build or claiming release readiness. The packet maps
all FT-01–FT-30 capability groups to their design anchors, delivery/verification
routes and missing implementation evidence. The canonical traceability and
activity manifests retain the full source-task and acceptance IDs.

For the fixed D01–D10 base-variant pilot, SP-125 requires at least 60 release
asset cells across main, alternate and slow demonstration, cue audio, captions
and setup, plus four pathway parent-guidance sets and exact-version rights and
coach approval. A01–A03 are accepted review samples only and do not certify
player-release cells. D11/D12 and additional variants remain unavailable until
separately approved and fully asseted.

Eight estimate gaps cover identity/recovery, web/API authorization, media/cloud,
iOS/device work, content/accessibility, safeguarding/support, billing and cash
runway. No custom quote exists. The unchanged workbook is an assumption-based
baseline, not a supplier price or approved budget. The AUD 0 discovery-spend
cap and founder-only external-contact authority remain in force.

`AC-SP-064-01` through `AC-SP-064-05` are accepted for this bounded planning
scope. R14, R19 and R20 retain the packet's later production, funding and
exact-build verification gates. SP-009's H1–H3/L1 findings, SP-012 controls,
physical device tests and real-child/release approvals remain separate open
obligations. This decision does not complete them.
