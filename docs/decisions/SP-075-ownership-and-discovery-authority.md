## Accepted decisions

1. **Accountable ownership:** Syed Ahmed owns product, technology, coaching, safeguarding, privacy, operations, release and delivery for the current one-person company. Multi-agent orchestration may prepare, implement, test and review bounded work, but agents act under Syed Ahmed's accountability and do not become human office-holders or approval authorities.
2. **Human backups:** Every backup/alternate position is explicitly vacant. Syed Ahmed cannot be his own backup. An agent is not a human backup, safeguarding alternate, recovery approver or independent signatory. Work that requires a second real person remains blocked until one is appointed and accepts the scope.
3. **Discovery budget:** The current discovery-spend cap is **AUD 0**. “Required” or “necessary” work does not override this cap. Any paid account, supplier work, device, service, travel, quote fee or other cost requires a new dated approval stating the amount, scope, currency, tax treatment, commitment owner and stop condition before the cost is incurred.
4. **External contact:** Only Syed Ahmed may contact specialists or suppliers and request estimates or quotes. Agents may use existing non-sensitive project material to research public information and prepare briefs or draft messages for Syed Ahmed. Agents may not send the messages, disclose non-public/sensitive material, accept terms, create paid accounts, recruit participants or incur costs without a later explicit authorization naming the party, scope and boundary.
5. **Discovery briefs:** No `DE-*` discovery brief in the [ACT-SP-075-01 review packet](../delivery/ACT-SP-075-01-ownership-and-discovery-review-packet.md) is issued now. Internal and agent-assisted work may continue within existing authority. The following external/specialist briefs become mandatory before the stated boundary:
   - `DE-02` qualified youth-coach/content review before youth training content is published or treated as approved;
   - `DE-03` Australian safeguarding/privacy advice before involving children in a pilot or processing their real data; and
   - `DE-06` independent security review before a child-data pilot or public release.
   Other briefs remain deferred until internal work identifies a concrete need or a downstream gate requires them.
6. **Independent review and alternates:** Genuine independent reviewers and alternates are currently vacant. A different real person must be engaged before any privileged recovery requiring dual review, before handling a safeguarding report involving Syed Ahmed, before independent security/pilot acceptance, and before relying on emergency operational access while Syed Ahmed is unavailable. These may be scoped external advisers or contractors; they do not need to be permanent employees, but they must be real, named, appropriately capable and accountable for the recorded task.
7. **Delivery consequence:** The vacancies do not block internal planning or ordinary bounded agent-assisted development. They do block the sensitive pilot, recovery, child-data, specialist-approval and release actions identified above. No work may claim those gates are passed merely because Syed Ahmed holds all primary roles.
