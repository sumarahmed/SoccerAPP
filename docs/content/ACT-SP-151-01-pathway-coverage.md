## Entry and progression rules

Every offered route requires:

- an approved, current exact variant;
- age suitability assessed separately from current ability;
- a coach-approved starting point based on attributed evidence;
- the stated supervision, equipment, space, assistance and assignment; and
- an eligible exact next action or exact easier alternative.

Progression requires coach-approved assessment evidence under the approved
setup. Practice completion and parent observation may be recorded but cannot
raise assessed ability or cause progression. Position relevance is advisory and
never blocks an otherwise eligible player.
