# ACT-SP-153-01 — Coach-selected skill protocols

Version `1.0-review-ready` records three small, non-normative checks from the approved D01–D10 catalogue.

| Protocol | Setup and result | Validity and assistance | Retest |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P01-D01-v1` controlled inside touches | D01 setup; two 20-second sets with 30-second rest; result is observed controlled alternating touches | Observer and assistance source recorded; stop on unsafe surface, pain, distress, or lost safe space | Not sooner than 7 days; conservative pilot default pending explicit final owner acceptance |
| `P02-D03-v1` deliberate dribble-stop | D03 setup; four journeys; result is observed journeys ending in a controlled stop | Cue-giver and assistance recorded; invalid if lane/setup or stop cannot be observed | Not sooner than 7 days; same limitation |
| `P03-D05-v1` gate pass | D05 setup; six attempts; result is valid ground passes through the gate | Partner/retrieval mode and observer recorded; invalid service or unsafe retrieval is excluded | Not sooner than 7 days; same limitation |

There are no age norms, pass/fail talent scores, rankings, or automatic pathway/ability changes. Recording is optional; a parent observation must be labelled as such and may omit details the observer could not reliably see.
