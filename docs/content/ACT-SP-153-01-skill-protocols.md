These checks support personal, repeatable observation. They do not create age
norms, rankings, pass/fail talent labels, professional-potential scores or
automatic pathway/ability changes.

## Approved protocols

### `P01-D01-v1` — Controlled inside touches

- Use the `D01.base.v1` setup: one ball and a clear, level approximately 2 × 2 m area.
- Complete one unscored 20-second familiarisation set, then rest/reset for 30 seconds.
- Complete two measured 20-second sets with 30 seconds between them.
- Record controlled alternating inside-foot touches separately for each set; retain raw counts without producing an age/talent score.
- A lost-control touch does not count and does not add replacement time. External interruption, wrong setup or an unobservable set is invalid.

### `P02-D03-v1` — Deliberate dribble and stop

- Use `D03.base.v1`: one ball, two flat markers and the recorded reference lane with safe run-off.
- Complete one unscored familiarisation journey, then four measured journeys with a reset after each.
- Record the actual lane length and whether the stop used a fixed mark or standard spoken cue.
- Record controlled-stop journeys from 0–4. An unsuccessful stop is a valid measured journey with a zero outcome; interruption, wrong route/setup or inability to observe the stop is invalid.

### `P03-D05-v1` — Ground pass through a gate

- Use `D05.base.v1`: one ball, two-cone gate and safe retrieval space.
- Complete one unscored familiarisation pass, then six measured passes with a reset after each.
- Record actual pass distance, gate width, ball, surface, footwear, foot/side and solo-retrieval or partner-retrieval mode.
- Record valid ground passes through the gate from 0–6. A missed gate is a valid measured attempt with a zero outcome; interruption, wrong setup, unsafe retrieval or an unobservable attempt is invalid.

## Shared validity and safety

- Assistance is recorded as `none`, `standard-verbal-cue`, `adult-setup`,
  `adult-retrieval`, `partner-service` or `physical-assistance`.
- Physical assistance during a measured attempt makes it non-comparable with an
  unassisted result. Partner/service modes are never assumed interchangeable.
- Allow at most one replacement for each externally invalid attempt. If the full
  measured set cannot be completed safely, record `NO_RESULT`.
- Stop for pain, distress, dizziness, an unsafe surface, obstruction,
  uncontrolled ball path or loss of safe space. This is not medical diagnosis.
- Formal comparable retesting is no sooner than seven days. Earlier coach
  observations may be stored as standalone results but are excluded from trend comparison.
- Recording is optional. Lack of video does not invalidate a sufficiently observed result.

Observation source is one of `coach-observed`, `guardian-observed` or
`adult-player-self-observed`. A child's reflection is stored separately and does
not become a formal skill-check result by itself. A guardian may report visible
counts and setup facts, but cannot certify technique, assessed ability,
progression, coach attribution or anything not directly observed.
