## SP-151 — Foundation learning pathways

**Required:** For every offered pilot pathway, record the learning goal, exact
approved starting variant, ordered approved variants, prerequisites, easier
alternative, progression condition, next step and short parent setup guidance.

**Recommended pilot shape:** Keep four pathways—ball control, passing, move and
turn, and receive/finish—and offer only a pathway whose complete exact variant
chain is within approved D01–D10 and has required demonstration coverage.
Missing/withdrawn cells make that path unavailable. D11 and D12 are reviewed
but deferred and are not offered in the initial catalogue.
Age suitability and ability are separate, and older beginners receive mature
language. SP-006 animation sample evidence is also a formal input.

**Recorded input:** The owner attests Aaron M approved the latest D01–D12 draft,
with D01–D10 as the initial release set. Actual A01–A03 samples now exist and
are provisionally owner-accepted; the structured SP-151 contract still waits

## SP-152 — Deterministic next-session rules

**Required:** A versioned, deterministic table using pathway, attributed
ability evidence, available time/equipment/space/assistance and current club
assignments. It must cover no equipment, limited time, missed session, younger
advanced player, older beginner and conflicting assignments.

**Recommended precedence:**

1. block withdrawn, unapproved or unsafe content;
2. enforce current age/supervision/ability and authorization constraints;
3. respect required equipment, space, assistance and available time;
4. expose conflicting club/home workload rather than combining it silently;
5. choose the next eligible item in the approved path;
6. otherwise offer one approved easier alternative; and
7. return an explicit no-recommendation reason when nothing is eligible.

Completion alone never increases assessed ability, difficulty or workload.

**Human decision needed:** Aaron M/product owner approve precedence, workload
boundaries, reassessment triggers and the treatment of conflicts.

## SP-153 — Repeatable skill-check protocols

**Required:** A small coach-selected catalog in which every result records the
protocol/version, setup, result unit, valid/invalid attempts, assistance,
conditions, observation source, retest interval and comparability decision.

**Recommended boundary:** Do not invent age norms, talent scores or technique
judgments. Recording is optional. Parent observation is labelled as such. A
changed ball, surface, dimensions, partner/service, assistance, side, protocol
version or material condition makes comparison unavailable unless the coach's
exact protocol permits it.

**Human decision needed:** Aaron M selects the small protocol set and supplies
the safe setup, units, valid attempts, rest/retest and comparability rules.

## SP-154 — Bounded coach feedback

**Required:** Deliberate adult submission to an assigned coach, current grant
checks for queue preview/playback, submitted/reviewed/withdrawn/expired states,
one structured cue, one approved follow-up and a text/no-video route.

**Recommended lean pilot:** No automatic upload or browse-all queue, no
unrestricted chat, no promise until a coach accepts the assignment, minimal
notifications and no report details in ordinary trackers. Clip length, open
queue capacity and response target remain explicit owner decisions rather than
invented service promises. Withdrawal/expiry immediately blocks new access.

**Human decisions needed:** Club/coach owner accepts clip limit, maximum open
submissions and staffed response wording; safeguarding/privacy owner accepts
visibility, notification and reporting boundaries.

## SP-155 — Healthy goals and reminders

**Required:** Optional coach-approved weekly goals, private milestones,
rest/early-stop/missed-week behavior, opt-out without loss of training, adult
reminder preferences, timezone/quiet hours and retry/duplicate handling.

**Recommended default:** Goals and reminders start off. An adult opts in for
the exact player. No rankings, public streaks, points economy, competitive
minutes target or recording requirement. Rest and early Stop count as safe
completion behavior; missed weeks use supportive return language and do not
erase earned milestones. Child-directed promotional reminders are excluded.

**Human decisions needed:** Aaron M/product owner supplies any numeric session
or workload limits; the product/safeguarding owner approves copy, quiet hours
and reminder frequency.
