## 2. Review outcome requested

For each drill and each releasable age/ability variant, the coach returns one of:

- **Approve** — exact version is suitable for the stated audience/context;
- **Approve with recorded correction** — correction is incorporated into a new version and that exact version is rechecked;
- **Restrict** — publish only the named subset of ages, abilities, assistance modes or variants;
- **Replace** — use a named reviewed alternative; or
- **Stop** — do not release because the setup, cue, progression, workload, demonstration or evidence is unsuitable.

Silence, general encouragement, review of a different version or approval of only the drill name is not acceptance. A reviewer may approve one variant while withholding another.

## 6. Cross-drill findings for coach disposition

1. **Ranges are not prescriptions.** Existing dimensions, repetitions and timings are production/usability starting points. A coach must approve ranges and stop conditions per releasable variant.
2. **Age and ability are independent.** An older beginner may use a beginner route without childlike language; a skilled younger player cannot bypass age/supervision suitability.
3. **“Equivalent” prerequisites need observable definitions.** The reviewer should state what demonstrates readiness when the named predecessor was not completed.
4. **Success criteria are qualitative drafts.** The reviewer must make them repeatable enough for SP-153 without turning them into unsupported performance scores.
5. **Harder/expert concepts are separate variants.** They remain unavailable until specifically reviewed; a general base-drill approval does not release them.
6. **Partner and rebound variants differ.** D05/D06/D10 service, ball path and safety cannot be approved as interchangeable without separate wording.
7. **Camera/media is optional.** Recording quality cannot be a condition for successfully performing a drill. Framing guidance must keep devices and people outside ball/run paths.
8. **D12 is intentionally narrow.** Diving, collisions, high balls, overhead service and more demanding goalkeeper work remain excluded from this pilot contract.
9. **No individualized prescription.** Suitability inputs and reviewed choices guide selection; the app and this packet do not diagnose, rank talent or replace a coach/health professional.
10. **Content rights remain open.** Written approval must bind the exact drill and asset versions, author/source rights and permitted use.

## 8. Repeatable skill-check protocol questions for SP-153

For each approved drill/variant, the coach should specify:

- exact setup and permitted range;
- number of familiarization attempts and measured attempts;
- same-side/both-side handling;
- assistance and partner/service standard;
- observable technique outcome(s);
- separate decision/accuracy outcome where applicable;
- invalid-attempt and reset rules;
- immediate stop/regression conditions;
- whether video is unnecessary, optional for the family, or explicitly shared for named review;
- minimum repeat interval and fatigue/rest boundary; and
- what constitutes enough evidence to progress, repeat or substitute without labeling talent.

No threshold in this packet is approved. The reviewer-supplied protocol must be versioned and linked before SP-151/SP-153 can treat it as input.
